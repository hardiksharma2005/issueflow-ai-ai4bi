# 6. A tour of the screens

The menu on the left of every page takes you to each part of the tool. Here is what you'll find on each page.

## Signing in
**Sign in** page: enter your email and password. There's a **"Forgot password?"** link if needed. New people can **create an account**, but they won't see any projects until an admin or manager adds them to one.

## Overview (the dashboard)
The first page after signing in. It greets you by name and shows:

- **Number tiles** along the top: projects, total issues, open, in progress, resolved, **overdue** (red) and **critical**.
- **Created vs resolved:** two lines showing how many problems came in and how many were fixed over the last 30 days. If the blue line rises faster than the orange one, work is piling up.
- **Resolution trend:** how many problems were fixed each week.
- **Issues by status, urgency and type:** simple bar charts.
- **Needs attention:** critical or overdue problems that are still open.
- **Recent imports:** the latest WhatsApp uploads.

Every chart has a small **table button** in its corner to see the exact numbers instead of the picture. You can choose **one project** from the box at the top right.

## Projects
A card for each project you belong to. Click one to see its issues, team members and uploads. Admins can create new projects; managers can add or remove team members.

## All Issues
The full list of problems, in two views (switch at the top right):

- **Table:** sortable columns, and filters for project, status, urgency, type, owner and dates. Shows 25 at a time.
- **Board:** each problem is a card in a column (Open, In Progress, Blocked, In Review, Resolved, Closed). **Drag a card** to another column to update it, or use the card's **⋯** menu → *Move to*.

Managers have a **New issue** button to add a problem by hand.

## One issue
Click any problem to see everything about it:

- title and description
- the details on the right: progress, urgency, type, owner, due date. Members can only change **progress**, and only on problems given to them.
- **WhatsApp messages** it came from, if it was imported
- **attachments**: photos and files
- **comments**
- **history**: who changed what, and when

## My Tasks
Everything assigned to you that isn't finished yet, **late items first**. The number next to "My Tasks" in the menu shows how many there are.

## Imports
The list of WhatsApp uploads, with their status: *Uploaded → Parsing* (reading the chat) *→ Parsed* (ready for the AI) *→ Processing* (AI is reading) *→ Needs review → Completed*, or *Failed* / *Duplicate*. **New import** opens the upload screen (see [document 4](04-whatsapp-processing.md)). Opening an upload shows the conversation, the photos, and the AI suggestions to review.

## Reports
Five ready-made reports: **Open issues**, **Overdue issues**, **Team workload** (how much each person has), **Project progress**, and **Issue resolution** (how long fixes take). Each has an **Export CSV** button to open it in Excel.

## Automations
The four automatic checks (see [document 9](09-automation.md)): when they run, when they last ran, and the result. Admins can **run one now** or switch it off.

## Team Members
Everyone in the workspace and their role. Admins can **invite people**, **change roles** and **remove people**.

## Settings
Your name, a link to change your password, light or dark colours, which AI the tool is using, and a short summary of how data is protected. Admins also see the **activity record** here.

## Things available everywhere
- **Search box** at the top (or press **Ctrl + K**): type any word to find projects, problems, comments or WhatsApp messages.
- **Bell icon:** your overdue problems, uploads waiting for review, failed uploads.
- **Sun/moon icon:** switch between light and dark colours.
- **Your initials** (top right): your account and **Sign out**.

## Works on phones too
Every page has been checked on a phone-sized screen. The menu folds away behind the **☰** button.

## Dates and times
All dates and times are shown in **India time**.
